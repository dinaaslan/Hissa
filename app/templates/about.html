Our story

What is حصة?

حصة is a simple way to book a tutor at a time that works for you.

In Arabic, حصة means a class session. That's exactly what we help you get.

Why we made it

Finding a tutor usually means sending a lot of messages. "Are you free?" It takes days just to set up one lesson.

We wanted to make that easier. With حصة, tutors add the times they're free, and families can see them right away and pick one. That's it.

Tutors set their availability once. Families and students see open times right away and book in a few clicks. No guessing, no double-booking, no wasted time.

Who it's for

Students & families

See when tutors are free, book a session, and keep track of your lessons in one place.

Tutors

Add your free times, change them whenever you need, and only get bookings when you're available.

What we hope for

We want to make learning easier to start. When booking is simple, students show up, tutors stay organized, and every hour counts.

Your next lesson is one click away.
